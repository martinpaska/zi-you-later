# Paintings behind Your path

Old Chinese ink landscapes from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, released by the museum as Open Access
(public domain, CC0). Each one is cropped to the painted area, turned into the app's ink and paper, and its edges fade
into the paper (`ink1.jpg` … `ink5.jpg`).
