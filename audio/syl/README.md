# Syllable recordings

1,616 Mandarin syllables in tones 1–4, one MP3 per syllable,
named `<syllable><tone>.mp3` with ü written as `v` (e.g. `lv4.mp3` = lǜ). `index.json` lists them.

**Source:** [audio-cmn](https://github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn) by Hugo Lopez (PLIDAM, INALCO),
`64k/syllabs`. **Speaker:** Chen Wang. **License:** [CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

**Changes:** leading and trailing silence trimmed, loudness evened out (−20 dB mean), re-encoded as mono 56 kbps MP3, `cmn-` prefix
dropped, `jv4` renamed `ju4`, the interjection files (`_hm`, `_m`, …) left out. Also left out after checking each file's pitch contour: the neutral-tone `…5` files (they hold seconds of audio, not one syllable) and 44 files whose tone does not match their name (e.g. `jia2` and `shan2` do not rise, `guo4` and `zhong4` do not fall). The app uses the device voice where a recording is missing. The files in this
folder are shared under the same CC BY-SA license.
